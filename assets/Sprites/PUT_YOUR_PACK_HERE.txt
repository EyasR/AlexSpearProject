Put the SpearFishing Assets Pack here.

1. Download the pack (https://nszym.itch.io/spearfishing-assets-pack) and unzip it.
2. Copy its "Sprites" folder into this "assets" folder, so you have assets/Sprites/Diver-32x32/...
3. Copy its "Licence.txt" into this "assets" folder too.

The pack's licence does not allow anyone to share its pictures, so this project comes without them.
Your own copy stays in your own project.
